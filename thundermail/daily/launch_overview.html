Research/repro tickets are the real cost center. Each individual reply isn't much slower than a routine ticket — the pain is rounds: users get bounced back and forth while we investigate. A ticket in this bucket waits roughly 4.1× longer in total than a straightforward one. Known-problem tickets, by contrast, tend to resolve fastest per-reply because the answer is already templated — the fix belongs to engineering, not support capacity.
Refund/Cancel is a known gap, already tracked: canceling or refunding via Paddle doesn't deactivate the Thundermail account, so support tracks these manually to know who to delete. Real fix belongs in account sync, not support capacity — thunderbird-accounts#1167 (refunded subscriptions left active/usable, with example tickets) and the broader thunderbird-accounts#176 offboarding epic.

Methodology & glossary

Complexity segments: Straightforward = never entered Hold status. Research/repro = entered Hold at least once, not linked to a tracked Problem. Known problem = ticket type Incident, linked to a Problem ticket.

Total wait / between-reply gap: computed from actual public comment timestamps (customer comment → next public agent reply), not ticket status — avoids the pending/hold/bump-macro distortion in calendar-time metrics.

Confirmed bug rate: % of a theme's tickets with a GitHub issue link (zd-gh tag) attached.

Ticket subjects and content are aggregated/categorized only — no raw customer text or PII in this report. Raw Zendesk export may show ~882 tickets before exclusions (test/merged/misrouted tickets removed).
