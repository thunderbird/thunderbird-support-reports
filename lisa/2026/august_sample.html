Engineering priority alignment · feedback vs August GitHub work

Live cross-check: August merged-PR report plus current issue states in thunderbird-android.

  • Work mix: 64 PRs merged across main/release; 2 Highlight + 17 Include are reportable. Most reportable work was composer, UI, localization, attachment, QR, and crash work.
  • Push — gap: Play Store #1 (34 negative) and K-9 Forum notifications rose 1→5. No targeted push/notification fix appears in August's Highlight/Include set. Open: #11056, #11083, #10079, #11057.
  • Spam — gap: Play Store #2 (18 negative). No August spam implementation PR; #1508 and #5676 remain open.
  • QR — aligned: Negative mentions cooled 25→3. PR #11248 added feedback for unsupported QR data in 24.0b1. Timing aligns; the data does not establish causation.
  • Crash — partial: Four negative reviews. PR #11309 fixed a camera/process-death crash, a narrow path rather than the whole crash theme.
  • Outbox — gap: Five negative reviews. No August reportable PR matched outbox/send failure; open issues include #2898 and #10006.
  • Other shipped value: HTML signatures #10876 and message-reader actions #11401 were August Highlights, but neither targets the top three review frictions.

“Merged” is engineering activity, not proof that a fix reached stable Play users in August. The report records beta/release metadata separately.

Why the non-AI report is quiet
  • The detector floors are eight questions of one kind in a day, six in a week, or eight in a month. Roland's August Android corpus holds 40 questions.
  • Only 2% of Android questions record a Thunderbird version, so the version-and-cause detector cannot fire at all — that zero is missing data.
  • Roland's own reading: a zero means "nothing large enough to fire", not "nothing happened".
